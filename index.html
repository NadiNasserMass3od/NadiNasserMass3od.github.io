About me

I'm a self-driven developer from Egypt with about two years of hands-on Flutter experience, building and shipping my own apps end to end: design, code, release and maintenance. My work isn't tutorial projects; it's software that real people use every day.

On the hardware side, my Electrical Engineering degree came with real firmware: PIC and AVR microcontrollers, motor drivers, UART and USB HID communication, and a graduation project that tracks objects with a motorized camera.

One project needs a clear note, because I believe in describing my role exactly as it was: the Church Services Schedule web app was my idea, and I defined what the churches needed, reviewed the AI-generated build screen by screen, tested it, tracked its usage and consumption, fixed issues together with the AI, and researched and chose the database, backend and hosting — PostgreSQL on Neon, a serverless backend, and Cloudflare Pages — so it could run live on the web. I did not write its code, and I do not claim database or backend expertise — my contribution was the idea, the requirements, the review, and getting it live and kept live for three churches.

Product & Delivery · Web App

Church Services Schedule

An installable Arabic RTL web app that shows the services and activities of three churches — Virgin Mary, Archangel Raphael, and St John the Beloved — in one daily timetable (6 AM–6 PM), with filters by church and date, a searchable activities list, a services directory, dark mode, and activity adding for authorised users. It is used daily by the three churches.

My role in this project was product and delivery — not code. I came up with the idea and defined the requirements, then reviewed the AI-generated build in detail: I went through the screens and the behaviour, checked usage and consumption, spotted problems, and fixed them together with the AI until the app worked the way the churches needed. I also researched and chose how it would run live on the web — a PostgreSQL database on Neon, a serverless backend, and hosting on Cloudflare Pages — and got it launched and kept it running.
